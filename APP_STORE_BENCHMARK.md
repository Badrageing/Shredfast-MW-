# App Store benchmark · 30 October 2026

Five established trackers selected for relevance to strength logging; not a verified current chart top five. Sources: German Apple iTunes Search API / App Store product descriptions, not hands-on native app testing.

- [Hevy - Gym & Trainingstagebuch](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/hevy-gym-trainingstagebuch/id1458862350?uo=4): 4.89/5 from 13121 ratings at retrieval.
- [Strong Workout Tracker Gym Log](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/strong-workout-tracker-gym-log/id464254577?uo=4): 4.82/5 from 10133 ratings at retrieval.
- [Fitbod Workout & Gym Planner](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/fitbod-workout-gym-planner/id1041517543?uo=4): 4.65/5 from 1741 ratings at retrieval.
- [Alpha Progression Muskelaufbau](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/alpha-progression-muskelaufbau/id1462277793?uo=4): 4.90/5 from 12135 ratings at retrieval.
- [JEFIT Gym Workout Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/jefit-gym-workout-tracker/id449810000?uo=4): 4.64/5 from 1312 ratings at retrieval.

Design decisions: Strong/Hevy inspire quick logging and automatic rest after a set; Alpha Progression inspires exercise-level history; Fitbod inspires equipment-focused session entry; JEFIT inspires searchable workout reference. No proprietary screens or assets copied.

Version 10 adds a next-session card, bottom navigation, consistent visual design, set confirmation with rest timer, exercise history and workout search. Checkmarks acknowledge entered sets and start rest; entered repetitions remain the source of truth for saving, as in version 9. No native Apple Health, Apple Watch, Live Activities or App Store distribution is claimed. The product remains a GitHub Pages web app.
